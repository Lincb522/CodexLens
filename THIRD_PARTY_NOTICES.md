## Tibo Watch

The optional Tibo reset-signal monitor adapts the public FxTwitter source
contract and deterministic `rules-v1.1.0` patterns from
[`Dopetaiga/tibo-watch`](https://github.com/Dopetaiga/tibo-watch), commit
`242735d0858c961b6d9f4db2468f114f02d8a71e`, with a separately versioned narrow
completion rule for observed “reset has been propagated” evidence.
